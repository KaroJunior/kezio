Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

What this policy covers

This privacy policy explains how Kezio handles information when you use the website. Kezio is a small independent project, and the website is designed to be as minimal as possible.

Information collected

Kezio does not have user accounts, so no personal information is collected or stored through the website itself. The site does not use cookies for tracking purposes.

The website uses localStorage to remember your theme preference (light or dark mode). This information stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere else.

Contact form submissions

If you send a message through the contact form, your name, email address, and message are submitted and delivered to an email inbox. This information is used only to respond to your message. It is not stored in any database or shared with third parties.

External tools and services

Kezio includes links to external tools, web apps, and Telegram bots. Some of these are built and maintained by the same person behind Kezio, while others may be operated by third parties. When you use an external tool, it operates under its own privacy policy. Check the privacy policy of any external tool you choose to use.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Any changes apply from the date they are posted.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach out through the contact page.
