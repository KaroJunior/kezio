Frequently Asked Questions

What is Kezio?

Kezio is a collection of web tools and Telegram bots built to solve everyday problems. Each tool is its own independent project.

Are the tools free to use?

Most Kezio tools are free to use. Some may have usage limits or different pricing, so check the individual tool before using it.

Can I suggest a tool?

Yes. Visit the contact page and send your idea. If it's something useful, it may become a future Kezio project.

Help keep them free and running. Your support makes a difference.
